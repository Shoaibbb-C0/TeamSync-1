About TeamSync

Connecting builders, one hackathon at a time

🎯

The Problem

Finding the right teammates for hackathons is challenging. Developers, designers, and product managers often struggle to connect with like-minded individuals who share their passion and complement their skills.

Many talented individuals skip hackathons simply because they couldn't find a team. Great ideas remain unbuilt, and potential collaborations never happen.

💡

Our Solution

TeamSync is a dedicated platform for hackathon participants to discover teammates, join teams, and build amazing projects together.

  • Smart matching algorithm based on skills and interests
  • Real-time feed of team requests and member searches
  • Comprehensive hackathon directory
  • Profile showcasing to highlight your expertise
  • Instant connection and communication tools
🚀

Our Vision

We envision a world where no one has to skip a hackathon because they couldn't find a team. Where every builder can easily connect with others who share their vision and complement their skills.

TeamSync is more than a platform—it's a community of innovators, creators, and problem-solvers coming together to build the future.
